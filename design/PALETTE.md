# SlaughterFest 2026 palette and vibe

Sampled from the client's own 2026 poster (title, frame, armour, skulls, panel). The client has since supplied the poster PSDs (`psd/`, git-ignored). Line-art elements from them (altar, mammoth, gate crown, reaper) are used as faint baked stamps; see README.

## Facts on the poster
- Saturday, 5 December 2026, The Humming Tree (Bangalore). Produced by Surgeculture.
- Partner strip: Catalyst Studio, Gigman Media, SkillBox, plus a fourth logo (name not confirmed).

Rules: ~80% void/ink/bone, one red moment per screen, copper and gold only as small accents. No neon, no gradients that glow, no pure white or pure black.

## Vibe
- Inked, engraved, aged: hand-drawn linework, crosshatching, soot, grain. Matte, never glossy.
- Funereal ceremony: a temple gate, a hooded figure, bone elephants, flags. Heavy, ritual, monumental, symmetrical.
- Type: the spiky festival wordmark as the only loud thing. Everything else quiet and formal: small-caps serif for dates and venue, wide letter-spacing, thin rules. Blackletter only as a sparing accent for band names.
- Details that fit: hairline frames, stone and carved-panel textures, barbed or cobweb line accents, a single red banner.
